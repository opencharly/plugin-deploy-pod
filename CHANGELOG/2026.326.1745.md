# 2026.326.1745 — feat(pod): wait for the deploy's declared readiness probe after start

## Summary

`spec.Deploy.Probes.Readiness` (spec/schema/deploy.cue `#DeployProbes`) declares a
per-deploy readiness Op, but nothing consumed it: `charly start` — and the `charly
start` at the end of `charly update` — returned as soon as supervisord's children
reported RUNNING. That is also true of a start wrapper still waiting on its database,
so a bed that probed right after the post-update restart raced the application:
`check-cachyos-immich-pod`'s `check-live-rebuild` ran ~3s after the restart and failed
`pg_isready` while the first live leg, with a longer head start, passed
(opencharly/charly#1234).

`podStart` now runs the authored readiness Op **inside the container** after the
container starts, bounded by the shared `poll.PollUntil` primitive, before returning.
Both the initial start and the post-update restart therefore hand back a deployment
that accepts work. The wait is separate from the acceptance checks: an acceptance
failure is evidence and is never retried here.

Only `command:` readiness probes are honored (the pod venue runs them with `sh -c`
inside the container). Any other verb is refused loudly rather than silently ignored.

Fixes opencharly/charly#1234 (cross-repo reference; the issue is closed by hand after
this merges).
